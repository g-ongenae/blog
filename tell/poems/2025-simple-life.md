# Simple life

🛝 <br>
As a teen, life is simple <br>
You just want to swing <br>
As hard and high as possible <br>
In a sunny garden

🕺 <br>
As a young adult, life is simple <br>
You just want to swing <br>
As loud and fast as possible <br>
In the pubs and the clubs

🙈 <br>
As a middle age, life is simple <br>
You just want to swing <br>
As hard and shady as possible <br>
In the private club

🏌️‍♂️ <br>
As a retired, life is simple <br>
You just want to swing <br>
As far and precise as possible <br>
On the green field

Life is simple, it's just swing!
