𝗜 𝗶𝗻𝘃𝗼𝗹𝘂𝗻𝘁𝗮𝗿𝗶𝗹𝘆 𝗺𝗮𝗱𝗲 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗯𝗲𝘀𝘁 𝗮𝗱 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗺𝘆 𝗰𝗼𝗺𝗽𝗮𝗻𝘆.

A few days ago I created myself a small tool to improve the UI of LinkedIn for my own usage.
I decided to share it with the world by posting a screenshot for demo purposes on LinkedIn.

So, I opened LinkedIn and I took a screenshot of the page.
And in it was my company's, Algoan, latest post.
I thought the visual was pretty so I was like, "Nice." and carried on.

I thought it was going to make a few hundred, or less, views like my other posts.

Well, I wasn't prepared for it to make more than 30,000 views.

Neither was my colleague.

You may think, "Oh but that's not such a big number."
But that's without taking into account that Algoan is a really niche and technical company. 
In our field we are really well known, but outside… 🙅‍♂️

We have big clients (really big) and small ones too.
You are using them everyday.
And you may have used our solution multiple times without knowing.

The sad law of a white-label product…

But it doesn't matter to us: our mission is to make credits more accessible and responsible.

Anyway, it's still great to be in the spotlight sometimes!
