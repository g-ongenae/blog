**Get it out of my face!**

One thing that really bothers me about most interfaces is how much completely useless information they throw at me.

LinkedIn is no exception.

Premium features I don't use.
Constant suggestions to follow new people.
Games on the side that I never play.
A huge "Start a post" section taking up valuable space.
"Show translation" buttons for languages I already speak.

How is any of this helping me?

**It isn't.**

So I made myself a small userscript (with Claude's help, much faster…) to declutter my LinkedIn home feed.

And it leave space on the side of the post for the images, instead of below.
I have a simple button to expand and collapse posts instead of that tiny "… more" link.

And all the unnecessary clutter? Gone.

Better, isn't it?
