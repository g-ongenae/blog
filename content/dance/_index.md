I've been interested in dancing for a long time.

When I was in high school, I watched the "[Dance Street]" dance battles
(seasons [1&2][dance_street_s1_2], [3][dance_street_s3], [4][dance_street_s4])
on [France Ô].

When I was at university, I took a couple of hours of classes to learn [four-count rock].

I often danced when I was alone, or drunk.
People would tell me then that I danced well.
I did not believe them (and I was _right and wrong_).

At the end of 2022, my real journey started.
Since then, I've been dancing a lot, and I mean a lot.

I've danced (in the order I started them, not by time spent):

- [Lindy Hop](@/dance/lindy-hop/_index.md) (100+ hours)
- [Charleston](@/dance/charleston/_index.md) (100+ hours)
- [Waltz](@/dance/waltz/_index.md) (10+ hours)
- [West Coast Swing](@/dance/wcs/_index.md) (50+ hours)
- [Solo Jazz](@/dance/jazz/_index.md) (50+ hours)
- [Balboa](@/dance/balboa/_index.md) (50+ hours)
- [Collegiate Shag](@/dance/shag/_index.md) (10+ hours)
- [Blues](@/dance/blues/_index.md) (50+ hours)

At first, I only danced as a leader.
In 2024, I also started dancing solo and following.

At some point, I started taking notes after my classes and sharing them on my [Strava].
Even though no one (except my sister's partner) read them…
I've talked with a lot of people about dance.
Both made me want to write everything down somewhere.
