# As your desire

If you want to be an engineer <br>
As your desire, <br>
You have to never surrender

If you want to be an architect <br>
As your desire, <br>
You have to never reject

If you want to be a man <br>
As your desire, <br>
You have to be the same

Try, If you want but <br>
The only fact important is the result

The result should be happiness
