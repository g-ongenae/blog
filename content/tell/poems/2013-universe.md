In general people baptize him God, <br>
But some people identify him differently. <br>
He is the thousand names man. <br>
Romans christen her GAÏA. <br>
Voltaire terms him the Big Architect. <br>
Umberto Eco spells it Nature.

I call it Universe.

The solar system is a small part. <br>
The Earth is a miniature portion. <br>
We are an undersized fragment.

I am a very small undersized piece of the Universe.

You too, so respect you. <br>
And respect the entire world.
