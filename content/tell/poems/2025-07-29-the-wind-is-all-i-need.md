The wind is all I need <br>
For it will mess with my hair <br>
For it will sing in my ears <br>
For it will bring the smell of flowers

The sun is all I want <br>
For it will light my view <br>
For it will warm my heart <br>
For it will shine all day

The wind is all I could get <br>
For it has left so soon <br>
For it has taken all sound <br>
For it has been felt

The sun is all I could wish <br>
For it has left a mark on my skin <br>
For its warmth has not withered hastily <br>
For the Moon will shine for it
