**When I am an old** man, **I shall** be mocker <br>
Like Nelson MUNTZ, I won’t have moral. <br>
**And I shall** go in the theater <br>
Like (a) mad man, I will shout my laugh. <br>
**I shall** be violent <br>
Like a small terror. <br>
The people will be afraid of me. <br>
I won’t make a difference between boys and men. <br>
**I shall** go in a market and won’t buy. <br>
I shall go in a café and won’t pay. <br>
I shall be old, criminal and very happy.

So, **you can** arrest me. <br>
You want me to say truth <br>
But I’m lying to you. <br>
So, you can intern me.

**But now we must** be serious <br>
And pay our rent, say only truth <br>
And be in good standing. <br>
**We must** be human <br>
**But maybe I ought to** <br>
Practice a little now? <br>
**When suddenly I am old,** I’ll be in prison.

_To Jenny Joseph_ <br>
_To Mrs Jones (English teacher)_
