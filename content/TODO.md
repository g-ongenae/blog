  Concepts
    Why we dance
      -> Sexual selection
      -> Good for you (mind and body)
      -> Expression
      -> Connection
    How to dance
      -> Conventions not rules => made to understand each other
      -> Music is the first partner
      -> Music creates movements, forced movements is not the way to go
      -> Watch, Steal, Improve
      -> Basics, Basics, Basics (master the basics)
      -> Forget the Basics (dance without thinking of doing specifics step that you've studied)
      -> Make it your own (do not get stuck in making it like it was)
      -> 4 Pillars of dance (Shape, Step, Space, Time) (Posture, Pulse, Pas)
    How to teach
      -> Practice (like a martial art)
      -> Explain
      -> Show
      -> Try
      -> Have fun
    Feeling the dance
      -> Energy of a dance => The dance shape the community
      -> Dancing Good & Dancing Bad
      -> Are we moving or are we dancing?
